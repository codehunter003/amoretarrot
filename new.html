FAQ

Frequently Asked

Do I need prior tarot experience?+
Not at all. Every session begins with a short conversation about what's on your mind, and your reader guides you through each card as it's drawn — no background knowledge required.
How are sessions conducted?+
Sessions take place over live video or voice call, with an option for chat-based readings if you prefer to keep things text-only. You'll receive a booking link after checkout.
Can I reschedule my appointment?+
Yes, appointments can be moved up to 24 hours before the scheduled time at no extra cost. Just reach out through the contact details in the footer.

What to Expect

Every reading is a conversation, not a performance — grounded, honest, and centred on what matters to you right now.

Sessions are recorded on request, so you can revisit the guidance whenever you need a reminder of the path ahead.
